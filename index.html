About us

A small, owner-managed restaurant company

Yers Ltd was set up on 19 June 2013 to operate in the licensed restaurant trade: restaurants that serve food alongside a licence to sell alcohol. The company is run by its director, Phenchan Wills, who has led it since incorporation.

More than thirteen years on, Yers Ltd remains an active, privately owned limited company, registered in England and Wales.

  • Licensed restaurant

    Our registered activity is the operation of licensed restaurants, bringing food and drink together under one roof.

  • Owner-managed

    One director has run the company since it was founded, so decisions are made close to the business.

  • Established 2013

    An active limited company since June 2013, with its annual accounts and confirmation statements on file.
